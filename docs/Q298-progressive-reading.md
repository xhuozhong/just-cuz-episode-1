# Q298 — reading preparation and bounded recovery

Q298 makes preparation state clearer and adds bounded background work while keeping the accepted comic sources and existing reading controls intact.

1. **Paper loading tabs on desktop and phone.** Compact tabs sit at panel edges and present loading, recovery, or manual retry guidance without covering the artwork with progress panels.
2. **A clear invitation when previews are ready.** Once the complete bound preview is verified and the protected panel is usable, its tab changes to “drag to try.” Percentages and HD-loading chatter disappear; the first actual drag dismisses the invitation. A partial preview or failed integrity check cannot declare completion.
3. **One automatic retry for transient frame failures.** A failed preview, selected frame Range, or explicitly selected fallback pack gets one 350 ms backoff and one retry for transport/5xx/header-timeout/body-idle failures. Header and streamed-body idle deadlines are eight seconds. Verified partial rows, current angles, and healthy sibling canvases remain intact. SHA, format, geometry, source-binding, 4xx, unsupported Range, and intentional cancellation never automatically retry. Hidden/offline/disposed state cancels recovery; terminal exhaustion exposes manual retry. Manual Range retry resets only the affected target’s budget. Existing reviewed delivery fallbacks remain; automatic recovery does not reset global mirror state or open an unsolicited HD set.
4. **Bounded idle prewarm and one gesture resume.** After the current native page and all current panels/selected angles are usable, one second of idle time may warm the next page in the reading direction. This requires known 4g connectivity, downlink above 2.25 Mbps, and no data-saving mode, gesture, dialog, hidden tab, failure, or recovery. Each wave admits at most a 512 KiB original plus 450 KiB of model/config/one preview; the preview itself is capped at 350 KiB. Two waves per page are allowed: the initial wave and one gesture-interruption resume. Completed bodies are retained. No speculative HD/video or second preview is fetched; visibility/connection changes do not grant another restart.
5. **Temporary phone reading preview with native handoff.** Cold phone loads may show a separately verified light WebP while the canonical JPEG prepares. Canonical loading regains high priority after the light attempt settles or a 1.5-second fallback; a stalled light attempt stops at six seconds. A reusable warmed original or known-native session within ten minutes skips this layer. The temporary layer never marks the original ready or feeds lettering, source protection, or interactive players.

The per-panel Enlarge buttons remain removed. Existing zoom, pinch, pan, fullscreen, language, reset, and panel interaction controls are retained. The 85 canonical originals and accepted art, frame/video, lettering, protection, crop, geometry, and source-binding contracts remain unchanged; the light images are separate reading derivatives.

Local validation covers loading UI, reading/native handoff and prewarm boundaries, 25 native Edge recovery cases, and six delivery regressions. The three merged recovery sources match their validated source hashes; owned recovery test browsers and servers are closed. Real public before/after measurement is still pending, so this release note makes no measured speed or performance-gain claim.
